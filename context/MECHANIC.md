# Mechanic

How the game plays. Keep it small: one core thing the player does, done well.

## Core loop

*What does the player do over and over?*

Example: "Sweep the beam, spot a ship, hold the light on it until it reaches the harbor."

>

## Controls

*Which keys or mouse actions, and what does each do?*

Example: "Mouse moves the beam. Click to flash it."

>

## Win and lose

*How does a round end? What counts as doing well?*

Example: "Lose when three ships hit the rocks. Score is ships saved."

>
