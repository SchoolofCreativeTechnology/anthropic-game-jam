# Starter Cards

Pick **one** card. The card sets the one action and a closed **Allowed** list: anything not listed is out. **Stretch** items wait until M4 (look) is done. You bring the world, the character, and the twist.

These limits are Nathan's, sized for what the jam models can build in a day.

## Every card

- Up to **3 models** (from `assets-packs/`, CC0 downloads, or headless Blender scripts)
- Up to **3 sound effects**
- Textures: canvas-drawn or small image files
- Input: keyboard, plus tap or click
- Title screen, score, game over, restart
- **Never:** multiplayer, saves or accounts, levels built by hand, story or dialogue, inventory or shop, physics engines, complex enemy AI, cutscenes

## 1. Lane runner

- **Action:** switch lanes (jump allowed as the one alternate input on the same obstacle stream)
- **Allowed:** endless track, one obstacle type, one pickup, speed rises over time, distance score
- **Stretch:** a second obstacle type

## 2. Jumper

- **Action:** tap to flap or hop
- **Allowed:** gaps or platforms scrolling, gravity, one hazard, score per gap
- **Stretch:** moving gaps

## 3. Single-screen shooter

- **Action:** aim and fire
- **Allowed:** fixed or slider position, one enemy type in waves, wave count rises, one projectile
- **Stretch:** a second enemy type

## 4. Arena dodger

- **Action:** move to dodge
- **Allowed:** one arena, one hazard type spawning from the edges, spawn rate rises, score is time alive
- **Stretch:** one pickup

## 5. Timing tapper

- **Action:** press at the right moment
- **Allowed:** one target (stack, catch, or beat), a timing window that tightens, combo counter
- **Stretch:** one visual beat cue

You bring: the world, the character, the twist.
